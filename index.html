Why choose Lingua Azur

Native teachers

Your teacher grew up speaking the language, so you learn how people really talk in the street, at the market and at work. They also know where learners usually get stuck, and they help you through it.

Small groups

We keep classes to 8 people at most, so everyone gets time to speak in every lesson. You are never just a face at the back of the room, and your teacher knows your name and your progress.

Flexible schedule

Choose mornings, evenings or Saturdays, whatever fits your week. If work or family gets in the way, you can move a class to another day without losing it.

Your learning roadmap

Every course follows the European levels, from A1 to C1. You always know where you are and what comes next.

  1. Step 1 · Start

    Free level test

    A 20-minute chat to find your level and your goals.

  2. Step 2 · A1 - A2

    First conversations

    Shopping, directions, introducing yourself.

  3. Step 3 · B1

    Daily life

    Doctor, town hall, work: handle it on your own.

  4. Step 4 · B2

    Confident speaker

    Discuss news and opinions, write clear emails.

  5. Step 5 · C1

    Exam ready

    Prepare DELF, Cambridge, DELE or CILS exams.

Questions

I am a total beginner. Can I join?

Yes. Most of our students start from zero, and our A1 groups begin every month.

What if I miss a class?

Tell us 24 hours before and you can join another group that week, at no extra cost.

Do you prepare for official exams?

Yes: DELF and DALF for French, Cambridge for English, DELE for Spanish and CILS for Italian.
